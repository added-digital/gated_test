# Dos and don'ts

These are 200 don'ts and 200 dos drawn from the record as of 2026-10-23. The
sources are `game/NOTES.md`, `game/IDEAS.md`, `sakura-kit/breaks.md`,
`SESSIONS.md`, the two concept passes, the two PINCH specs, the ladder doc and
the conversations that produced them. Each line stands alone and is numbered,
so a session can cite "Don't 81" or "Do 143". Both lists use the same ten
sections in the same order. Add new lines at a gate, not mid-rung.

Sections: 1 The ladder and the gates · 2 Ideas, scope and kill lines ·
3 Working with the designer · 4 PINCH, the loop · 5 PINCH, the dream, the
frame and the readouts · 6 Characters, dialogue and tone · 7 Look, art and
the kit · 8 Building in Godot · 9 Tests, verification and evidence ·
10 Sessions, git, agents and tools.

# Part one: 200 don'ts

## 1. The ladder and the gates

1. Don't spend on a later rung's question before the current rung has its proof.
2. Don't call a rung done because its time box ran out; it is done when its proof exists.
3. Don't extend a box; when it runs out, pass, shrink or kill.
4. Don't pass a player gate on the designer's own enjoyment; someone else must have played the build.
5. Don't let a bot's scripted route stand in for a person's play at any gate.
6. Don't let the local session act as the confidant; it cannot be held, bored or accomplished.
7. Don't accept "done" without the command that ran, its output, and the running game seen.
8. Don't write the Goal sentence for the designer; the AI interviews and offers no sentence of its own.
9. Don't let the AI pick the verb; it lists candidates when asked and never chooses.
10. Don't change the Goal, the verb, the engine, the feel or the pipeline mid-rung without a written kill line first.
11. Don't start a toy without its hypothesis line: "I believe … because …; dead if not by …".
12. Don't write a hypothesis that cannot fail.
13. Don't move a kill date because the build is nearly there.
14. Don't treat the schools' stage names as the rule; the rule is the order of the questions.
15. Don't let a failed gate restart the project or swap the engine; a failed gate shrinks scope.
16. Don't hold a gate open until Friday; a gate passes when its proof exists, and Friday only logs it.
17. Don't start a rung without its testers booked.
18. Don't mix the build calendar (build days 1 to 5, A to C) with the dreamer's nights; say which one you mean.
19. Don't design the whole game on paper while the loop is unproven; that is Macro, three rungs early.
20. Don't let "AI makes it cheap" justify skipping a question; cheap building is the strongest invitation to skip.
