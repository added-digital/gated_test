- 2026-10-22 · PINCH, the loop, designer's words after playing day 4 · "What I dislike about it is that ideas must be caught. In Dave the Diver you can swim upwards to the surface at any time to end the swim, but it comes at a cost: two swims a day, and if you don't get enough fish you don't make the customers happy at night. Bringing up a set amount of ideas is counter to this 'free but costly' way of exploring. Rather than a continuous dream, each room has options (the ideas) to traverse further into. If the idea is Bo's pills you have to actively travel towards that idea, and that shapes the next room. With a visible map that's a choice for a night: go down and explore his dream. If you don't succeed you don't return with anything valuable. If you succeed you get the idea for pills, which when developed on the surface unlocks something you can do in the dream world. The surface can't be that I just interact with people up top. It must be more contained, like a sushi bar night: 'study sleep', some interactive element very different from travelling through dream rooms." → the rebuild (toy 4b): a map on the Long House wall, one target per night, branching rooms, the idea as the goal room, scraps on the way, a free self-pinch to wake, the Desk as the surface burst
